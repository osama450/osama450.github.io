About

Senior engineer, production-first

What I do, where I've done it, and what I'm accountable for.

I'm a Senior Flutter Developer based in Giza, Egypt, with more than five years building cross-platform mobile products. Most of that work has been in domains where reliability isn't optional — national rail ticketing, last-mile logistics, and regulated real-estate investing.

My current focus is production quality at scale. On Saudi Arabia Railways' passenger app I drove crash-free sessions to 99% across a 3M+ user base, built the operator app from scratch on Clean Architecture, and hardened both with SSL pinning, code obfuscation and root/jailbreak detection.

I've led distributed mobile teams — running code reviews, setting coding standards, and owning App Store and Play Store release cycles automated with Fastlane and GitHub Actions. Alongside client work I co-founded Ikoja Sushi, where I own the full stack in Flutter, Node.js and Firebase as tech lead and product owner.

Outside of product work I help organise the Flutter Egypt community and contribute to open-source Flutter packages.
